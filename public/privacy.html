Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Holiday Learning Portal (https://holiday-learning-portall.netlify.app) lets schools share learning materials, assignments and online exams with their students during the holidays, and lets parents follow their child's work. This page explains what information the portal keeps and how it is used.

Information we collect

Teachers, principals and the site owner sign in with Google or with a code sent to their email, so the portal receives their email address and name. Principals also give the school name and a phone number, and teachers give their phone number and subjects. Teachers add their students' names, admission numbers and classes. The portal stores the materials, assignments and exams teachers post, the work and exam answers students hand in, the marks and feedback teachers give, questions students ask, and the checks and notes parents leave. Students and parents do not give an email address; a parent PIN chosen by the student is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form.

How the information is used

The information is used only to run the portal for each school. Teachers see the students and work of the classes they teach, principals see their own school, students see only their own work and marks, and parents see only their own child's work. The site owner approves schools and keeps the service running and backed up.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information or share it with advertisers. Data is stored with Supabase (database, file storage and sign-in) and the site is hosted by Netlify. These providers process the data only to operate the service.

Google sign-in

If you sign in with Google, the portal requests only your name and email address. It does not access your Gmail, contacts, files or any other Google data.

Keeping and deleting information

Information is kept while the school uses the portal, so that students' work and marks remain available. A school's principal can remove teachers and students, and a school can ask the site owner to remove the school and its data. To request deletion or a copy of your information, email linusomwoyo948@gmail.com.

Security

Each person can see only what their role allows. Files are shared through links that expire after a few hours, data is transferred over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and a backup is made every night.
